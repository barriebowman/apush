# APUSH

Instructional materials for AP U.S. History: interactive lessons, review exhibitions, and classwork built around the course's key concepts.

**[Launch page](https://barriebowman.github.io/apush/)**

## Building Slavery: A Colonial Exhibition

An interactive, walk-through exhibition on the origins of British colonial slavery (1619 to 1740), built for Key Concept 2.6. Six rooms of primary source objects, a legal document archive, and a companion classwork booklet.

Available in two formats. [Walk in 3D](https://barriebowman.github.io/apush/building-slavery-3d.html) is a first-person, walk-through museum: best on a laptop or desktop with a keyboard, mouse, and a modern browser. [Browse in 2D](https://barriebowman.github.io/apush/building-slavery-2d.html) is a click-through gallery walk: works on any device, including tablets and Chromebooks.
