Building Slavery: A Colonial Exhibition

An interactive, walk-through exhibition on the origins of British colonial slavery, 1619 to 1740. Six rooms of primary source objects, a legal document archive, and a screening room. Available in two formats: a first-person 3D museum, or the original 2D gallery walk.

Walk in 3D → First-person, walk-through museum. Best on a laptop or desktop with a keyboard, mouse, and a modern browser. Browse in 2D → Click-through gallery walk. Works on any device, including tablets and Chromebooks.
